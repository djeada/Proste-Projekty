## About the Project

A digital version of the classic Yahtzee dice game. Roll five dice up to three times per turn and try to score combinations like three of a kind, full house, or the coveted Yahtzee (five of a kind).

## Requirements

* A modern web browser, such as the most recent versions of Chrome, Firefox, or Safari.
* There are no other libraries or frameworks required.

## Installation

1. Clone or download the repository.
2. Open `src/index.html` in your web browser.

## Usage

1. Click "Roll Dice" to roll the five dice.
2. Click on dice to hold/unhold them before the next roll.
3. You get up to 3 rolls per turn.
4. After rolling, click on a scoring category to record your score.
5. Try to maximize your total score across all categories.

## Features

* All standard Yahtzee scoring categories.
* Hold dice between rolls.
* Roll counter (up to 3 rolls per turn).
* Score calculation and total display.
* Visual dice representation.
